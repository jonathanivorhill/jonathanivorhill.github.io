THE WORLDS ARE REAL

Every ring holds a planet at its heart — and they're the real ones. Earth is NASA's Blue Marble. Jupiter turns up once per run, enormous, with the Great Red Spot parading past. Mars, the Moon, Europa, Io and more fly with their true faces, built from NASA imagery and the maps scientists actually use.

ONE TAP Your star orbits a ring. Tap to sling it across the void and catch the next one. Easy to learn, brutal to master.
CATCH FIRE Reverse your orbit ring after ring to build a slingshot weave and set the sky burning.
CLIMB THE WORLD A world leaderboard that celebrates you — rank climbs, podium ceremonies, medals you keep while you hold them.
